# Future roadmap and open items

Written 2026-11-24 when the user parked Sprint 15c. Each item below needs its own sprint (brainstorm, design, plan, build, review) in the usual way.

## The user's wishlist (in the order given)
1. **New maps and bosses** (planned as Sprints 16a, 16b and 16c in `sprint-development.md`). Same enemies on each map, drawn with different sprites per map (a per-map art set, not new enemy types).
   - An **open-field map** (no walls, Vampire Survivors style, with points of interest) and its boss, the **Tempest Dragon** (a Thunder boss).
   - A **full-maze map** (one entire maze, with points of interest) and its boss, the **Dire Minotaur** (a Land boss).
   - The home screen's Play button becomes a map select. Maps and bosses are already data (`MapData`, `MapMaze`, `BossData`, `BossPhase`, `BossAttack`); the new work is the map layouts, the per-map enemy art sets (`EnemyArchetype`/`EnemyElement` plus a sprite-set choice on the map, `SpriteLibrary` lookups by name), the two bosses' art, attacks and phases, audio, and balance.
2. **BUILT (Sprint 17, 2026-11-25): Achievements.** Twelve data-driven achievements with a listening tracker, a toast, a chime, a home-screen screen and the three boss spells (slots 3, 4, 5); see `docs/architecture/progression-and-economy.md`. (Original plan: defeating a boss rewards a starting spell; the saved bests and the `RunStats` tally were the groundwork.)
3. **A livelier home screen and tutorial** (planned as Sprint 18). More lively art for the main menu and the tutorial pages (today the home screen is a small title, a small wizard and a column of buttons, and the tutorial pictures are single 1:1 sprites). Needs new pixel art through the art pipeline (`art/sprites/*.sprite`, `tools/art/art_builder.gd`): a title treatment or banner, an animated scene behind the menu, and illustrated tutorial pages. Art taste to keep (feedback memory): bright, simple, cartoon, one pixel size, no scaling or rotating.

## Open items carried over from earlier sprints
- **FIXED (2026-11-25, awaiting the next publish): no sound on the GitHub Pages build.** Root cause, found by running the exported build in Chromium and WebKit with the audio graph instrumented: the Sound Board added the Music and Sfx buses at run time with `AudioServer.add_bus()`. In Godot 4.7.2 a web export plays sounds as samples through its own bus graph, and a bus added that way (always position -1) is moved to the wrong place by the engine script, which reverses the bus order; the sends then formed a loop, and Web Audio mutes a loop, so the output level was exactly 0. Fix: the buses come from `default_bus_layout.tres` (loaded at start), and the board never adds buses or sets sends (`tests/test_audio_buses.gd`). Measured after the fix in both engines: audible output, mute silences it and unmute restores it. Not measured: a real phone (an iPhone's silent switch still mutes Web Audio, which is a device setting).
- **Death card "Level reached"** (user feedback 2026-11-24, decided): the row and the saved best level always equal the permanent level, so the row is removed (with the saved best level) in Sprint 16a, together with the move to per-map bests.
- **Zone balance:** the spell audit (`godot --headless -s scripts/dev/spell_audit.gd`) found the four Zones do about 5 to 6 times the median damage per mana pool and 5 to 10 times the damage per second of the other Areas in a crowd. Nothing was changed, because the user decides from play.
- **Slow devices:** on a throttled CPU the 300-enemy scene swung from about 100 fps to 9 to 45 fps (the laptop was in Low Power Mode, so no trustworthy before and after exists). The game may lose frame rate sharply rather than gradually. A graceful-degradation design (fewer enemies, a cap on physics catch-up) is a user decision and needs real phone numbers first.
- **Phone testing:** no real phone has been used for any sprint; touch was checked in real windows with synthesised touches only. Testers should use the performance overlay.
- **Deferred minors** (see `development-log.md`, the follow-ups after each sprint): the 14c, 15a and 15b review minors (a failed save at death still shows this run's best, some tutorial text is hard-coded, the Tutorial pictures' invisible shadows, the home screen's full screen choice reapplied on every load, the F3 overlay toggle not saved, the pause menu's `load()` calls, and similar).
- **Exit warning:** a headless run that exits from the home scene logs an "ObjectDB instances were leaked at exit" warning that the run scene does not; a shutdown-order message, not investigated.
- **Older balance questions:** boss timing, Staff boss damage, point-of-interest balance (see `development-log.md`); the "massive maze mode" idea (a memory note) is covered by the full-maze map above.
