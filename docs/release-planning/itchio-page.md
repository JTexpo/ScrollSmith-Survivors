## Title

## Tagline
Craft your own spells. Survive the horde.

## Description
ScrollSmith Survivors is a pixel-art survival roguelite. A lone wizard faces wave after wave of goblins, wolves and worse, and the only way to win is to build better spells than the horde can handle.

Spells are not picked from a list, you forge them. Collect scrolls from fallen enemies, then combine a Fire, Thunder, Land or Sea base with a Line, Cone, Area or Missile shape and any buffs you find, at a Lectern in the middle of the fight. Each spell has its own mana pool that never refills by itself, so every cast counts.

Fight through three maps (a forest, ruins and a huge maze), each with its own enemies and a boss: the Ember Titan, the Tempest Dragon and the Dire Minotaur. Earn XP and Gold, level up your wizard, buy robes, hats and wands, and unlock twelve achievements.

Free to play in your browser, on a keyboard, a controller or a phone with touch controls. Progress saves in your browser.

## Controls
| Action | Keyboard and mouse | Controller |
|---|---|---|
| Move | W, S, A, D | Pad stick, Pad button 11, Pad button 12, Pad button 13, Pad button 14 |
| Aim | - | Pad stick |
| Cast | Mouse left | Pad stick |
| Interact | E | Pad button 0 |
| Previous spell | - | Pad button 9 |
| Next spell | - | Pad button 10 |
| Inventory | I | Pad button 3 |
| Next potion | - | Pad button 2 |
| Previous potion | - | Pad button 7 |
| Drink potion | - | Pad button 1 |
| Pause | Escape | Pad button 6 |
| Mute | M | - |

Touch: the left stick moves and the right stick aims and casts.
Touch: tap a spell slot or a potion to use it, Interact beside a Lectern, shrine or merchant.
Keys 1 to 5 pick a spell slot and Z X C V B N drink the six potions.

## Features
- Forge your own spells from scrolls: four elements, four shapes, many buffs
- Three maps, each with its own look, enemies and boss
- Bosses that warn you before every attack, so a fast enough player can always dodge
- A 20-level wizard, an equipment shop and potions from a roaming merchant
- Twelve achievements, with a starting spell for each boss you defeat
- Keyboard and mouse, controller and touch controls
- Bright pixel art and chiptune music, all made for the game
- Free, with no ads
